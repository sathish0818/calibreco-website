# Calibreco Technology — keyword map

Derived from the company profile deck: services, instruments, standards,
industries and location. Grouped by search intent.

Note: there is **no `<meta name="keywords">` tag** on the site, and there should
not be. Google has ignored that tag since 2009 and it only advertises your
target terms to competitors. Keywords work by appearing in real copy, headings,
alt text and structured data — which is where these are implemented.

## 1. Brand / navigational

Lowest competition, highest intent. Owned via title tag, header, footer, schema.

- calibreco
- calibreco technology
- calibreco chennai
- calibreco metrology

**Risk:** Google broad-matches "calibreco" to "Calibre" — a crowded name
(Calibre Technologies, CALIBRE Systems, Calibre Technology Australia). Fixed by
entity signals off-site, not on-page: Google Business Profile, LinkedIn,
IndiaMART, consistent spelling everywhere.

## What on-page work cannot do

The site is **one page**. It cannot rank well for "3D scanning services",
"laser tracker calibration" and "reverse engineering Chennai" simultaneously —
they compete for the same URL. The next real step is a page per service, each
600+ words with project photos and its own title, H1 and schema.

Ranking also needs off-page signals the site cannot supply on its own:

1. Google Business Profile (highest return for a local B2B service)
2. LinkedIn company page
3. IndiaMART / JustDial listings
4. Genuine links from customers, suppliers and industry directories
